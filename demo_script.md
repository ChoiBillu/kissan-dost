# 🎤 3-Minute Demo Pitch Script: KisaanDost

> Follow this exact script during your 3-minute stage presentation.

---

### [0:00 - 0:20] Part 1: Problem (20 seconds)
**Speaker:**
> *"Assalam-o-Alaikum judges and fellow hackers! Faisalabad is the agricultural hub of Pakistan, yet millions of rupees in crop yields are lost each season to diseases like Cotton Leaf Curl Virus and Wheat Rust. Farmers often can't access an agricultural officer in time, and existing research is locked in complex English papers. Today, we built **KisaanDost** to bridge that gap."*

---

### [0:20 - 1:50] Part 2: Live Demo (90 seconds)
**Speaker & Screen:**
> 1. *(Click the preset button on screen)*: *"Here, a farmer notices that their cotton crop leaves are curling upwards with visible whiteflies. With one click..."*
> 2. *(Show Gemma 4 output)*: *"Notice how Gemma immediately diagnoses Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV), pinpoints the whitefly as the carrier, and produces actionable advice in both English and natural Urdu."*
> 3. *(Highlight treatments)*: *"It gives exact active ingredients available in local pesticide shops—like Pyriproxyfen and Diafenthiuron—along with safe spray schedules and preventive agronomic steps."*
> 4. *(Highlight robustness)*: *"We also built a one-click offline backup mode into the app, ensuring zero crashes even under stage Wi-Fi fluctuations."*

---

### [1:50 - 2:20] Part 3: How Gemma is Used (30 seconds)
**Speaker:**
> *"KisaanDost is powered by **Gemma 4** (`gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`) accessed through the Gemini API. We engineered an agricultural pathology prompt that instructs Gemma to act as an expert agronomist, cross-referencing botanical symptoms against regional Punjab crop conditions and generating dual-language structured markdown cards."*

---

### [2:20 - 2:40] Part 4: What's Next (20 seconds)
**Speaker:**
> *"Next, we plan to connect KisaanDost to a WhatsApp voice gateway, allowing rural farmers to send a 10-second Punjabi or Urdu voice note and receive spoken audio advice straight to their phones. Thank you, and we'd love your questions!"*
